# Resilia — 3-Minute Hackathon Demonstration Script

**Theme:** *Maintaining vital community lifelines when networks fail.*  
**Target Time:** 180 Seconds (3 Minutes)  
**Presenter:** Full-Stack & AI Engineer

---

### [00:00 - 00:30] Problem & Product Vision
- **Screen:** *Home Screen (`/`)*
- **Speaker:**
  > "During Typhoon Ondoy and Ulysses in Marikina City, cell towers flooded and power grids failed. When disasters strike, community coordinators, residents, and responders are cut off from cloud servers right when they need life-saving coordination the most.
  >
  > Welcome to **Resilia**. Tagline: *Maintaining vital community lifelines when networks fail.*
  > Resilia is a network-resilient, agentic community disaster platform designed for Philippine communities. Notice our live status bar: River Warning Alarm Level 2 on Marikina River, with actual stored data for shelters, relief goods, and hazard reports."

---

### [00:30 - 01:15] Complete Disconnection & Offline Capabilities
- **Action:** Click **"Test Offline"** button in header.
- **Screen:** Status changes to `OFFLINE`. Amber banner illuminates.
- **Speaker:**
  > "Let's pull the plug. We just cut all network connectivity. Notice the app doesn't crash or show a blank spinner.
  >
  > Let's navigate to the **Emergency Map**. Even with zero internet, our embedded local cartography of Barangay Tumana and Malanday renders with zero tile drops. You can inspect Malanday Elementary School, road blockages, and flood zones.
  >
  > Now, let's test our AI and information layer under **Ask Resilia**.
  > Let's ask: *'Which evacuation center is accessible to wheelchair users?'*
  > Resilia immediately retrieves local records and answers that Malanday Elementary School has ground-floor wheelchair ramps and backup generator power.
  > Notice Resilia is completely honest: It does not pretend to run Gemini in the cloud; it explicitly labels this as local grounded retrieval and cites verified evidence IDs."

---

### [01:15 - 02:00] End-to-End Field Reporting & Priority Triage
- **Action:** Switch demo role to **"Resident (Lola Rosa)"**. Go to **"Get Help"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > "Now watch how offline data creation works. Lola Rosa is 76 years old and in a wheelchair as floodwaters reach her house.
  > She submits an evacuation request voluntarily disclosing wheelchair needs.
  >
  > Resilia writes this directly into IndexedDB, generates an immutable UUID event, and applies transparent priority rules: giving this a 95/100 Critical score based on elderly age, wheelchair mobility limitations, and Level 2 river alarm."

---

### [02:00 - 02:35] Coordinator Mission Approval & Responder Dispatch
- **Action:** Switch demo role to **"Coordinator (Capt. Maria)"**. Go to **"Dashboard"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > "As coordinator, Capt. Maria sees the AI Mission Proposal generated by the Needs and Coordination Agent.
  >
  > Following Product Principle 5: *Consequential actions require human approval.* The AI matched Lola Rosa's request with a motorized rescue rubber boat and Malanday School's ramp, but cannot dispatch responders automatically.
  > Capt. Maria clicks **'Approve Proposal'**.
  >
  > Now let's switch to **'Responder (Officer Juan)'**. Under **Missions**, Officer Juan sees his assigned rescue route, accepts the mission, moves it to *In Progress*, and completes the safe extraction."

---

### [02:35 - 03:00] Reconnection & Conflict-Free Cloud Synchronization
- **Action:** Click **"Simulating Offline"** toggle to reconnect. Go to **"Sync Center"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > "Now the storm clears and cellular network returns.
  > The Sync Agent detects connectivity, pushes all pending IndexedDB events to Google Cloud Run, receives idempotent server acknowledgements, and preserves audit trails.
  >
  > Resilia proves that community disaster response doesn't have to break when connectivity fails.
  > **Resilia: Maintaining vital community lifelines when networks fail.** Thank you!"
