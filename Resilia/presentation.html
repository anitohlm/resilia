Executive Summary

Brief about the idea

AI-Assisted Community Disaster Coordination Operating Under Severe Network Degradation

The 72-Hour Blackout Crisis

Category 4/5 typhoons drown cell towers and submerge power substations. When the internet dies, traditional cloud apps display blank loading spinners, leaving isolated families on rooftops unable to call for help.

Hybrid Dual-Engine Solution

  • Online Cloud Mode: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash synthesizes multi-incident reports and translates Tagalog/English.
  • Offline Edge Fallback: 15 client IndexedDB stores and deterministic rules execute locally without cloud round-trip delay.
  • Human Gatekeeper: Strict anti-hallucination evidence citations; rescue dispatches require human coordinator sign-off.

Comparative Advantage & USP

Opportunities

Addressing Critical Gaps in Existing Disaster Technologies

How different is it from any of the other existing ideas?

  • Local-First Edge Durability: Unlike cloud-dependent 911 portals that freeze during power blackouts, Resilia maintains 15 IndexedDB stores locally on client devices.
  • Tri-Role Operational Separation: Purpose-built cockpits tailored for Residents (reporting & beacon), Responders (GPS & START triage), and Coordinators (incident truth & ration ledgers).
  • Anti-Hallucination Grounding: Never invents safe routes; strictly cites verified emergency evidence IDs.

How will it be able to solve the problem?

  • Sub-Second Local Latency: Eliminates loading spinners; citizen reports commit immediately to local edge storage.
  • Transparent Vulnerability Prioritization: 0–100 scoring prioritizing bedridden seniors, infants, and mobility-impaired residents.
  • Relief Aid Integrity: Tamper-resistant QR barcodes with 24-hour distribution cooldown prevent duplicate aid claims.

USP of the proposed solution (Unique Selling Proposition)

Hybrid Dual-Engine AI with Zero-Compute Edge: Seamless transition between Google Cloud Gemini 3.8 Flash for macro intelligence and deterministic local arithmetic for micro-survival operations, requiring zero cloud compute spend at the edge.

System & AI Architecture

Architecture diagram of the proposed solution

Hybrid Dual-Engine Architecture: Google Cloud Platform + Resilient Edge Runtime

Google Cloud Platform (Macro Core)

  • Google Gemini 3.8 Flash (@google/genai SDK): Multi-incident synthesis, situation summaries, and Tagalog/English translation.
  • Google Cloud Run: Containerized Node.js service hosting REST endpoints (/api/v1/telemetry, /api/v1/sync).
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Distributed document datastore for city-wide disaster coordination and historical archives.
  • Google Maps Platform: Geospatial mapping, flood polygon overlays, and route verification.

Resilient Edge Runtime (Micro Core)

  • Browser IndexedDB (resilia_local_db): 15 versioned object stores persisting citizen reports, shelters, and relief logs.
  • Local Deterministic Agent Orchestrator: Mathematical vulnerability scoring (0-100), keyword lookup, zero-overdraft arithmetic.
  • Service Worker & PWA Cache: Pre-caches application shell for instant boot even under total cellular blackout.

Durable Reconnection & Sync

Idempotent Sync Agent: UUID transaction journal, SHA-256 conflict hashes, and exponential backoff health probing (/api/v1/health).
